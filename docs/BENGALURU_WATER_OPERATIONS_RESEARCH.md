## 2. Water Demand vs. Available Supply Dynamics

1. **Daily Water Demand**:
   - Total estimated gross demand: **2,100 to 2,600 MLD (Million Litres per Day)** based on standard urban norms of 135–150 LPCD (Litres per Capita per Day) + commercial/institutional demand.
2. **Cauvery Surface Water Supply**:
   - **Cauvery Water Supply Scheme (CWSS)** Stages I, II, III, and IV (Phases 1 & 2): **~1,450 MLD** pumped from T.K. Halli (~95 km distance, lifting water over 500 meters gradient).
   - **Cauvery Stage V Project** (Inaugurated / commissioned late 2024): Designed to bring an additional **775 MLD** specifically targeting the 110 outer villages across Mahadevapura, Bommanahalli, Yelahanka, RR Nagar, and Dasarahalli.
   - Total theoretical bulk surface supply capacity: **~2,225 MLD**.
3. **Groundwater Dependence & Crisis**:
   - An estimated **400,000 to 500,000 borewells** operate across the city (commercial, private residential, and municipal public borewells).
   - Core city areas (South, West, Central) have ~85–90% piped Cauvery coverage.
   - Outer tech-corridor zones (Mahadevapura, Bommanahalli, Whitefield, Bellandur, Sarjapur) historically suffered from high groundwater dependence (up to 50–70% of daily domestic usage supplied via borewells and private water tankers).
   - Water table depletion: Exceeds **1,000 to 1,800 feet** in peripheral zones, with thousands of dry borewells during summer months.
4. **Recycled and Treated Wastewater**:
   - Installed Sewage Treatment Plant (STP) capacity: **~1,200+ MLD** across 30+ major STPs operated by BWSSB.
   - Koramangala & Challaghatta (KC) Valley and Hebbal & Nagawara (HN) Valley projects pump ~400–450 MLD of secondary treated wastewater to drought-prone rural lakes in Kolar and Chikkaballapur for groundwater recharge.
   - In-situ reuse: Mandatory dual-piping for toilet flushing and landscaping in apartments having >20–50 units.

## 3. Distribution Infrastructure & Non-Revenue Water (NRW)

- **Transmission & Storage**: Bulk water reaches Master Balancing Reservoirs (MBRs) at High Grounds, Tataguni, and Mount Joy, and feeds **60+ Ground Level Reservoirs (GLRs)** and Overhead Tanks (OHTs).
- **Distribution Pipe Network**: Over **10,500+ km** of distribution pipelines, ranging from vintage cast iron (CI) pipes laid decades ago in the pete/core areas to ductile iron (DI) and high-density polyethylene (HDPE) lines in modern extensions.
- **Intermittent Supply Regime**: Water is supplied on an intermittent schedule (typically 2 to 4 hours every alternate day per sub-district valve zone).
- **Physical Contamination Risk (The Back-Siphonage Phenomenon)**:
  - When distribution mains are depressurized between supply cycles, a partial vacuum (negative hydraulic pressure) occurs.
  - If surrounding soils are saturated by leaking underground sewerage lines or roadside storm-water drains (SWDs), sewage and dirty water are drawn into the drinking water pipes through loose joints and micro-cracks.
  - When supply is restored, the initial flow contains a concentrated plug of turbid, contaminated water.
- **Non-Revenue Water (NRW / UFW)**:
  - Historically measured between **35% and 42%** of total input due to physical leakages, aging distribution pipes, unauthorized unmetered tappings, and commercial metering inaccuracies.

## 4. Water-Quality Testing and Public Health Surveillance

- **BWSSB Testing Regime**: Central Water Quality Testing Laboratory at High Grounds + Divisional Testing Laboratories.
- **Standard Applied**: **IS 10500:2012** (Indian Standard for Drinking Water — Specification).
  - Turbidity: Acceptable limit **1.0 NTU**, Permissible limit in absence of alternate source **5.0 NTU**.
  - Free Residual Chlorine: Minimum **0.2 mg/L (ppm)** at point of delivery; typical reservoir dosing **0.5 to 2.0 mg/L**.
  - pH: **6.5 to 8.5**.
  - Total Dissolved Solids (TDS): Max **500 mg/L** (acceptable), **2,000 mg/L** (permissible).
  - Bacteriological: E. coli or thermotolerant coliform bacteria **must be absent in any 100 mL sample**.
- **Public Health Disease Surveillance**:
  - Integrated Disease Surveillance Programme (IDSP) under the Directorate of Health & Family Welfare Services, Government of Karnataka.
  - BBMP Health Department monitors daily cases of Acute Diarrheal Disease (ADD), Typhoid, and Viral Hepatitis A/E reported by Urban Primary Health Centres (UPHCs) and referral hospitals.
  - Seasonal peaks occur during pre-monsoon showers (pipe scouring and sewage ingress) and peak summer (tanker contamination and borewell stress).

## 5. Administrative Jurisdiction & Multi-Agency Responsibility Matrix

| Operational Problem | Responsible Primary Authority | Supporting / Coordinating Authority | Official Reporting & Escalation Channel | Evidence & Verification Required |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Suspected Pipeline Contamination (Dirty/Smelly Water)** | **BWSSB** (Executive Engineer - Maintenance / Zonal Quality Lab) | **BBMP** (Zonal Health Inspector for UPHC area) | BWSSB 24/7 Helpline (1916), BWSSB Sahaaya App, Zonal Control Room | Street address, geocoded photo of tap sample, on-site chlorine/turbidity swab, certified lab report |
| **Pipeline Leakage / Burst Distribution Main** | **BWSSB** (Sub-division Assistant Executive Engineer) | **BBMP** Road Infrastructure / Traffic Police (if arterial road affected) | BWSSB 1916, Ward Valve Operator dispatch | Geo-tagged photo/video, street landmark, estimated pressure/flow drop |
| **Water Supply Interruption / Zero Pressure** | **BWSSB** (Zonal Valve Operations & Maintenance) | Local Ward Administrative Council | BWSSB Grievance Portal, Local Sub-division office | Locality name, duration (>24h), affected RR (Revenue Register) numbers |
| **Water Quality Testing (Formal Sample)** | **BWSSB Central Water Testing Lab** | Public Health Institute (PHI, Bengaluru) / KSPCB accredited labs | Official Laboratory Request through Sub-division AEE | Chain of custody sample bottle (1 L sterile), collection timestamp, residual chlorine check |
| **Suspected Waterborne Disease Cluster (ADD / Gastro)** | **BBMP Health Dept** (Chief Health Officer / District Surveillance Officer) | **BWSSB** (Chief Engineer - Quality Assurance) | Integrated Disease Surveillance Programme (IDSP Karnataka), UPHC Outbreak Log | Anonymized case linelist (symptoms, ward, onset date), spatial clustering analysis, water sample test result |
| **Commercial Tanker Price Gouging & Quality Violations** | **Bangalore Urban District Administration / DMA** | **BWSSB** (Tanker Registration Cell) & Police | District Collectorate Toll-Free Helpline, BBMP Control Room | Tanker registration number, delivery challan, TDS/source verification |
| **Sewage Overflow Ingressing Drinking Water Points** | **BWSSB** (Waste Water Management Wing) | **BBMP** (Storm Water Drain - SWD Wing) | BWSSB Sahaaya, BBMP Sahaya 1533 | Geo-coordinates, photo showing sewer overflow proximity to drinking lines |

## 6. Real-World Integration & Provenance Strategy

1. **Four-Tier Data Provenance Taxonomy**:
   - `REAL_DATA`: Verified live sensor streams, official BWSSB SCADA telemetry, or accredited laboratory reports.
   - `DEMONSTRATION_DATA`: Synthetic records calibrated with physical hydraulic equations to represent Bengaluru's intermittent operating reality safely.
   - `PUBLIC_DATA`: Open government data, census datasets, KSPCB monthly lake reports, published master plans.
   - `UNVERIFIED_REPORTS`: Citizen grievance submissions prior to field engineer triage or laboratory sampling.
2. **Operational Safety Philosophy**:
   - The platform acts as a high-fidelity **Decision Support & Early Warning System**. It never bypasses statutory field verification, clinical diagnosis, or lab testing before issuing public alerts.
